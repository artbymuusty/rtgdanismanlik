## Rules for adding more

- Source only from Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash, or Pexels — verify the license on the file's own page before downloading, not just from a search result.
- Accept only: CC0, Public Domain, CC BY (any version), CC BY-SA (any version). Never use an image whose license page shows "personality rights", NC (non-commercial), or ND (no-derivatives) restrictions.
- Record every new file in the table above at the time it's added.
- Never source a photo of an identifiable person to stand in for a fabricated student or mentor name/quote. Place, architecture, and anonymized (no visible face) human-presence photography only, for exactly the reason explained above.
- Real, consented photos of actual RTG students/mentors always replace anything here — when that happens, remove the corresponding row.
