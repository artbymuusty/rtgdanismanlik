# Photo credits

Internal reference file — not shown to site visitors. Every photograph in
`public/images/` is sourced from Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash, or Pexels
under a license that permits commercial reuse.

Two different uses of "real people" appear below, and they're governed by
different rules:

1. **Place/atmosphere photography and anonymized (no visible face) human
   presence** — `hero/berlin-hauptbahnhof.jpg`, `germany/humboldt-campus.jpg`.
   None depicts a named RTG student, mentor, or client, and no real
   person's likeness is attached to a fabricated name or testimonial.
2. **Generic editorial/illustrative photography of visible people** —
   `people/contact-conversation.jpg`, `people/mentor-consultation.jpg`.
   These *do* show identifiable faces, exactly like any consulting or
   education website's stock/editorial photography does to set a scene
   ("a conversation," "a consultation"). They are never captioned with a
   name, a quote, or any claim that the person shown is RTG staff, a
   mentor, or a student — that distinction is what keeps them apart from
   rule 1's "no visible face" requirement, which exists specifically to
   stop a stranger's photo from being passed off as a *specific* RTG
   person. If a caption or attribution ever needs to name someone in
   connection with either of these two files, replace the file first.

## Rules for adding more

- Source only from Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash, or Pexels — verify the license on the file's own page before downloading, not just from a search result.
- Accept only: CC0, Public Domain, CC BY (any version), CC BY-SA (any version), the Unsplash License, or the Pexels License. Never use an image whose license page shows "personality rights", NC (non-commercial), or ND (no-derivatives) restrictions, or an Unsplash+/Pexels-paid tier.
- Record every new file in the table above at the time it's added.
- Never source a photo of an identifiable person to stand in for a fabricated student or mentor name/quote, or to illustrate a mentor/student profile card — see the two-category note above for the one narrow exception (generic, uncaptioned editorial photography) and why it's different.
- Never use an AI-generated image, a stock preview/watermarked image, or a generic city/landmark photo chosen only because "it's in Germany" — the image must explain the section it's in, not just decorate it.
- Real, consented photos of actual RTG students/mentors/team always replace anything here — when that happens, remove the corresponding row.
- If no real, relevant, high-quality, properly licensed image can be found for a section, prefer no image (a strong text-led composition) over forcing a weak or irrelevant one in.
